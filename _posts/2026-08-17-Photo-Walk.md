I've taken a few photo walks recently and wanted to share my photos. They're purely aesthetic and don't mean much, but they were fun to take and fun to edit!

## Graveyard
Here are some photos of Fulton Street Cemetery from a couple months ago. I feel a bit bad taking a dead person and using them for my own personal gain (the gain being the joy of taking a photo). Hopefully they don't mind.

{% include img.html src='/assets/images/photo-walk-aug-2026/graveyard-01.jpg' %}
{% include img.html src='/assets/images/photo-walk-aug-2026/graveyard-02.jpg' %}

These were taken headphone-free. Something about a graveyard makes me want to put on an air of respect, which is probably why I felt a bit awkward taking these and put the headphones away.

At the same time, I think a stroll through a graveyard purely for pleasure is perfectly acceptable; the unkempt shrubbery from this cemetery gives a nice buffer from the noise of traffic. But I draw the line at headphones, hammocking, and throwing a frisbee for a dog. I honestly don't know where the line should be, though. This poorly maintained cemetery suffers more from fallen trees than from hippie hammockers.
