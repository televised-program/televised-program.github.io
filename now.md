## _travel_

## _reading_
Currently reading _Wind-Up Bird Chronicle_ by Haruki Murakami. It has very funny lines like "All I knew about Malta was the Herb Alpert performance of 'The Sands of Malta,' an authentic stinker of a song."

Last book I read was _Inherent Vice_ by Thomas Pynchon. I really liked it! Every once in a while he writes in these run-on sentences that, to read, feel much like the thoughts that meander through my head.
